# Responsible Use Statement

Troika D is developed to help people capture, record, document, teach, demonstrate, troubleshoot, and preserve legitimate activity on their own computers and in other contexts where recording is lawful and appropriate.

The project encourages responsible use. In particular, users are asked to respect privacy, consent, confidentiality, intellectual-property rights, and applicable law, and to avoid using Troika D for conduct such as unlawful surveillance, harassment, coercion, fraud, blackmail, unauthorized collection of private information, or other activity intended to cause harm.
