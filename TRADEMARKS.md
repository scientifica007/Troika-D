This policy is intended to preserve a clear distinction between the official Troika D project and modified, third-party, or unofficial distributions.

## Uses that are generally welcome

You may use the words "Troika D" truthfully to:

- refer to the Troika D project;
- describe compatibility or interoperability with Troika D;
- link to the official repository;
- discuss, review, teach, document, or report on the software;
- state accurately that another work is based on, derived from, or compatible with Troika D.

You may redistribute an unmodified official release while identifying it accurately as Troika D, provided you do not falsely imply a relationship beyond that distribution.

## Modified or third-party versions

If you distribute a modified version, fork, or derivative work, do not present it in a way that is likely to make users believe it is an official Troika D release or is endorsed by the Troika D project.

A clear descriptive statement such as **"based on Troika D"** or **"a fork of Troika D"** is appropriate when accurate.

For a substantially modified product, use a distinct product name and visual identity unless you have explicit permission to use the official Troika D branding.

## Logo and visual identity

Do not use the official Troika D logo or visual identity in a way that falsely suggests that a modified or unrelated product is an official Troika D release.

The logo may be shown for truthful editorial, documentary, compatibility, attribution, or project-reference purposes where the context does not imply endorsement.

## No effect on GPL rights

This trademark policy does not restrict copying, modification, distribution, study, or use of GPL-licensed source code. It concerns only the use of project identifiers and branding in ways that may create confusion about source, affiliation, or endorsement.
